KPI Anomaly Detection
Agent

A fully automated system that monitors 12 business KPIs every day, detects unusual activity, diagnoses the root cause, quantifies financial risk, and delivers the right alert to the right person — before it becomes a crisis.
Execution Summary

What This System Does

Every day, thousands of data points flow in: orders placed, revenue earned, website visits, ad spend returns, inventory levels, customer returns. Spotting a genuine problem — versus normal daily noise — manually is impossible at scale. This agent does it automatically. It reads all the data, applies three independent detection methods, cross-checks them, finds the root cause, calculates how much revenue is at risk, and routes a complete briefing to the right team — including three specific recommended actions — within minutes of data arriving.

The system covers 2 years of daily data (731 days, 12 KPIs across 6 categories), has been validated against 20 known anomaly events, and all five pipeline layers have been built and tested end to end. An AI orchestrator (Layer 6) coordinates the full pipeline and writes the daily executive brief.

How the System Works — 6 Layers
01
Data Foundation
Collecting & Preparing Business Data
Every day, the system automatically pulls together transactions, marketing spend, web traffic, and inventory records — 731 days of history across 33 business metrics. For each KPI, it computes "context signals": what was normal last week? Last month? How volatile has it been? These signals feed every step downstream. Think of it as building a fingerprint of what "normal" looks like for each metric — so any deviation from that fingerprint stands out clearly.
Revenue & Orders Marketing ROAS & Clicks Web Traffic & Conversion Rate Inventory & Return Rate
02
Anomaly Detection
Three Independent Alarm Systems — At Least Two Must Agree
Rather than relying on a single check that could generate false alarms, three independent methods run simultaneously. A statistical baseline catches sudden deviations from recent patterns. A machine learning model flags days when multiple KPIs move unusually together. A forecasting model compares each KPI to what it predicted. An anomaly is only confirmed when at least 2 of the 3 methods agree — 75.5% of KPI-day combinations are flagged as entirely normal, and only 8.3% of confirmed anomalies earn the top severity level by having all three methods agree.
HIGH: All 3 agree — 8.3% of anomalies (15 events) MEDIUM: 2 agree — 50.8% of anomalies (92 events) LOW: Watch list — 40.9% of anomalies (74 events) Suppressed: External cause — 3.3% (6 events)
03
Root Cause Analysis
Not Just "What" — But "Why"
Once an anomaly is confirmed, the system follows the chain of causation. If revenue drops, did it happen because orders fell, or because order value fell? If orders fell, was it because traffic dropped, or because the conversion rate declined? The system also checks external conditions — was a competitor flooding the market? Was consumer confidence low? Anomalies driven purely by market forces are flagged as "externally driven." Of the 181 detected events, 47.5% are routed immediately for action, 40.9% go to a watch list, and just 3.3% are suppressed because nothing can be done about them internally.
ESCALATE: 8.3% — all 3 methods agreed INVESTIGATE: 47.5% — actionable, 2 methods agreed MONITOR: 40.9% — lower-tier KPIs SUPPRESSED: 3.3% — market-driven, nothing to act on
04
Intelligence Engine
Financial Impact + Priority Ranking + Recommended Actions
Every confirmed anomaly is translated into a dollar figure: how much revenue is at risk over the next 7 days if the issue persists? That figure is scored alongside KPI importance, confidence in the diagnosis, and how fixable the problem is — producing a unique priority rank from 1 to 181. Three specific actions are then generated: what to do in the next hour, what to fix this week, and how to prevent recurrence. The 101 highest-priority events (55.8% of anomalies) receive AI-enhanced recommendations with full business context.
Revenue at risk quantified per anomaly Priority ranked 1–181, no ties 3 specific actions per anomaly AI-enhanced for 55.8% of events (101 of 181)
05
Communication
Right Alert, Right Person, Right Channel — Automatically
Every anomaly is routed to the correct audience via the correct channel. Immediate crises (8.3% of anomalies) go to executives and operations via Slack and email. Actionable events (47.5%) go to operations and analysts via daily email. Lower-priority signals (40.9%) appear in a weekly analyst digest. Market-driven events (3.3%) are logged silently — no noise, just an audit trail. Structured HTML reports with root cause, impact, and recommendations are generated automatically. A Power BI star schema is prepared for visual exploration by leadership.
ESCALATE → Slack + Email → Exec & Ops — Immediate INVESTIGATE → Email → Ops & Analyst — Daily MONITOR → Digest → Analyst — Weekly Power BI Dashboard (in progress)
06
Agent Orchestration
The AI Brain That Coordinates Everything
A Claude AI model acts as the central coordinator. It triggers every morning (or on new data arrival), decides which tools to call and in what order, interprets results at each step, and writes the daily executive brief in plain language. It holds the full business rulebook — KPI definitions, alert routing logic, the playbook index, and the dependency map — so it can make context-aware decisions without human intervention. The agent connects the five pipeline layers into a single coherent, automated workflow.
